Home Assistant on an e-ink screen

A calm, always-on control panel for your lights, climate and scenes, with the weather, bin day and a small pet that keeps you company.

Runs as a KUAL extension, in any browser, or as the page behind your e-paper dashboard. Open source, on your own server.

Install guide Source code

Made for paper

Black and white, big touch targets, one screen per page. Only what changed is redrawn, so taps stay quick on slow e-ink. Rooms and devices come straight from Home Assistant.

Pets and styles

Pick a pet and give it a name. It says good morning, notices cozy lights and reminds you about the bins.

Set up from any browser

Choose the cards, nicknames and icons, approve new devices, and drag cards around in the live preview.

More screens

Add a screen to the kitchen or the hall in a minute. Each one can have its own room, cards and pet, or simply follow the main settings.

No KUAL device? Any browser or e-paper dashboard can show it from a link.

  • Screens pair once with a private link.
  • The settings page needs a password.
  • Other websites can't press your buttons.
  • Locks and garage doors only open if you allow it.
  • Everything stays on your own network.

Safe on a wall

Anyone can tap a wall screen, so PaperKiosk is locked down by default.
